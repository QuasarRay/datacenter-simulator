# Learning route

Start with [the operator and visual language](courses/C00.md). Complete C01–C20 before P01. Projects assume the whole blueprint; exercises begin after their corresponding projects. The written route and planning examples are available. Live NVIDIA product qualification and a privileged Incus host remain required for full mastery.
